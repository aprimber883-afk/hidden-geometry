How to read our site

We worked as a group of three and built one shared website, but each of us picked our own pattern, did our own math and made our own creation. Every page follows the same three steps:

1 · Notice + NameWhat we saw in the real world, why it caught our attention, and a picture so you can see it too.
2 · ExplainThe math behind it, shown with diagrams, graphs, tables, formulas and words.
3 · CreateSomething new we made from the idea. Each creation is interactive, so you can play with it.

What connects our three stories

All three are about patterns that repeat by a rule. The honeycomb repeats a shape in space, so the rule is about angles and walls. The piano and the harp repeat sounds, and because sound comes from strings, the rule for sound turns into a rule for shape. In every case the math explains something you can see with your eyes.
